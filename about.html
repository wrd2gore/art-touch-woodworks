Master Carpentry & Architectural Joinery

At Art Touch for Wood Works, we specialize in custom woodwork, architectural interior fittings, and bespoke furniture crafted to precise architectural specifications in Amman, Jordan.

Led by General Manager Mohammad Y. Shaheen and CEO & Plant Manager Mohammad S. Maghari, our team manages the entire fabrication process from shop drawings to precision machining and final installation.
