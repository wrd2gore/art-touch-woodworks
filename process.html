The Art Touch 7-Step Framework

A rigorous, predictable, and transparent lifecycle engineered for zero tolerance and flawless turnkey delivery.

Phase 01
01

Initial Consultation & Laser Site Survey

Every project begins with an extensive review of architectural intent. Our site survey technicians take sub-millimeter 3D optical laser scans of raw openings, walls, and floor levels to capture real-world tolerances and MEP clearances.

Deliverables: Digital site scan report, preliminary budget range, timeline roadmap.
Team Involved: General Management & Survey Engineers.
Phase 02
02

3D BIM Engineering & Shop Drawings

Our design engineering division creates fully dimensioned AutoCAD & Revit shop drawings detailing joinery profiles, internal core construction, concealed hardware pockets, cable raceways, and expansion gaps.

Deliverables: Comprehensive shop drawing package & hardware specification book.
Team Involved: Design & 3D Engineering Team.
Phase 03
03

Material Curation & Physical Mockups

Clients and architects inspect physical timber samples, bookmatched veneer sheets, and bespoke lacquer swatches under controlled lighting. Physical 1:1 joinery corner mockups are approved prior to mass fabrication.

Deliverables: Signed material sample boards, veneer bookmatching layout approvals.
Team Involved: QA Division & Plant Procurement.
Phase 04
04

Precision Workshop Fabrication

Fabrication takes place in our Amman plant using automated 5-axis CNC routers, precision edge-banding, and hand carpentry joinery. Hardwoods are acclimatized in climate staging bays to guarantee dimensional stability.

Deliverables: CNC-milled structural components, pressed veneers, hand-finished joinery.
Team Involved: Fabrication Team & Mohammad S. Maghari (Plant Manager).
Phase 05
05

Dry-Fit Pre-Assembly & Multi-Point QC

To eliminate on-site surprises, complex cabinetry, architectural wall cladding, and oversized pivot doors undergo complete 100% dry-fit pre-assembly on our factory floor under independent QC inspection.

Deliverables: Factory QC sign-off certificate, protective crating clearance.
Team Involved: QC (Quality Control) Team.
Phase 06
06

Turnkey On-Site Installation

Our in-house master installers execute clean, protected on-site fitting utilizing optical laser alignment, hidden mechanical fixings, and seamless perimeter shadowlines.

Deliverables: Zero-defect site installation, clean handover walkthrough.
Team Involved: Installation & Logistics Team.
Phase 07
07

5-Year Warranty & After-Sales Care

Our relationship does not end at handover. Art Touch provides an official 5-year structural warranty, wood care guidelines, and on-demand maintenance for seasonal climate adjustments and finish touch-ups.

Deliverables: 5-Year Warranty Certificate, Care Manual, Dedicated Support Contact.
Team Involved: After-Sales & Service Department.
