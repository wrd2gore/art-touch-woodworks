Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

Art Touch Woodworks & Architectural Joinery ("Art Touch", "we", "our") respects the privacy and proprietary intellectual property of our clients, architects, interior designers, and general contractors. We treat all shared architectural blueprints, CAD files, 3D renderings, and project specifications with strict confidentiality under bilateral non-disclosure standards.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our website, contact forms, or 5-Step Quote Wizard, we may collect:

  • Contact details (Full name, company name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number).
  • Project metadata (Project type, site location, dimensional estimates, target completion date, budget range).
  • Uploaded design files (CAD drawings, PDFs, images, reference documents).

3. How Your Information Is Used

All submitted data is utilized strictly for:

  • Preparing accurate joinery cost estimations and technical proposals.
  • Conducting optical laser site surveys and coordinating project delivery.
  • Fulfilling our 5-year craftsmanship warranty records.

4. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

Art Touch does not sell, rent, or lease client data to any third party. Your CAD drawings and property details are accessible only by authorized members of our Design, QA, and Executive Management teams led by Mohammad Y. Shaheen and Mohammad S. Maghari.

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to request an NDA agreement before submitting files, please contact us at info@arttouchjo.com.
