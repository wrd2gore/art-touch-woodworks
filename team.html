Organizational Framework

Specialized Manufacturing & Quality Divisions

Our seamless organizational hierarchy ensures clear accountability, strict QA verification, and zero tolerance for defect across all project stages.

Design & Engineering Team

Translates architectural sketches and CAD files into 3D BIM models, shop drawings, and cutting optimization sheets.

  • 3D CAD & BIM Modeling
  • Joinery Section Details
  • Structural Load Verification
  • Hardware Integration Specs

QA (Quality Assurance)

Establishes strict material grading standards, timber moisture testing, and vendor certification protocols.

  • Raw Timber Moisture Audits (8-10%)
  • Hardware Cycle & Load Testing
  • Vendor Quality Standards
  • Process Compliance Audits

Fabrication Team

Master carpenters and CNC operators handling precision 5-axis milling, veneer matching, and joinery assembly.

  • 5-Axis CNC Precision Milling
  • Bookmatched Veneer Pressing
  • Solid Wood Carving & Profiling
  • Factory Dry-Fit Assembly

Finishing & Coating Team

Operates dust-free pressurized spray booths for polyurethane lacquers, hand stains, and custom patinas.

  • Pressurized Dust-Free Spraying
  • Hand-Rubbed Stains & Oils
  • Fire-Retardant Sealers
  • High-Gloss & Ultra-Matte Coatings

QC (Quality Control)

Conducts rigorous sub-millimeter dimensional, tolerance, and finish inspections before packaging.

  • Sub-Millimeter Tolerance Check
  • Surface Sheen & Color Match
  • Hardware Alignment Check
  • Protective Packaging Sign-Off

Installation Team

Dedicated on-site master installers equipped with optical laser levels and clean-site protocols.

  • Laser-Level Optical Positioning
  • Seamless Hidden Fastenings
  • Dustless On-Site Fitting
  • Turnkey Handover Walkthrough

After Sales & Service

Provides dedicated post-handover support, routine maintenance, seasonal wood care, and 5-year warranty fulfillment.

  • 5-Year Craftsmanship Warranty
  • Seasonal Climate Adjustments
