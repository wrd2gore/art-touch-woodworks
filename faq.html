General & Design

General Inquiries & Design Drawings

Art Touch specializes in bespoke custom woodwork, architectural wall and ceiling paneling, luxury custom furniture (dining tables, boardroom suites, credenzas), high-end kitchens, oversized pivot doors, acoustic timber baffles, and complete turnkey commercial, hospitality, and residential interior fit-outs.

Materiality & Engineering

Hardwoods, Veneers & Climate Resistance

All raw timber imported into our plant is kiln-dried and acclimatized in our climate-controlled staging bay to achieve optimal equilibrium moisture content (EMC 8–10%). We utilize engineered cross-grain substrate cores, internal steel tension rods, and moisture-resistant multi-coat polyurethane sealers to eliminate warping or joint separation.

We work with prime American Black Walnut, European White Oak (Crown & Rift cut), Ash, Natural Teak, Iroko, and exotic bookmatched veneers. We also supply MDF, Marine-Grade Plywood, High-Pressure Laminates (HPL), and custom painted finishes matched to any RAL/NCS code.

Pricing & Handover

Quotations, Lead Times & 5-Year Warranty

Quotations are itemized based on scope of work, timber species, hardware mechanisms (e.g. Blum, Hettich, FritsJurgens), and finishing complexity. Standard commissions typically range from 4 to 8 weeks depending on project scale. Preliminary quotations are provided within 24–48 hours via our Quote Wizard.

Art Touch provides an official 5-year craftsmanship warranty covering structural integrity, mechanical hardware, and joinery bonding. Our dedicated After Sales & Service team is available for routine touch-ups, seasonal adjustments, and refinishing services.
